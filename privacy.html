Privacy Policy

This is a prototype draft for the product build. It describes how Skilvi Technologies Ltd handles personal data on the Platform.

1. What we collect

Account data: phone number (required), email (optional), name, and profile content you add (bio, portfolio, photos). Transaction data: orders, payments, wallet ledger, withdrawals. Verification documents: government ID and self-photo, collected only when you apply for the verified badge. Usage data: pages viewed, searches, messages metadata (not message content, except where needed for dispute resolution).

2. Why we collect it

To operate the marketplace: matching, escrow, payouts, dispute mediation, and fraud prevention. We process identity documents solely to issue the verified badge and for regulatory compliance. We do not sell personal data, ever.

3. How we store it

Card data never touches our servers — payments are tokenised by our payment provider (PCI-DSS scope stays with them). Verification documents are encrypted, access-restricted to the verification team, and deleted 12 months after the verification decision. Transaction ledgers and audit logs are retained for at least 2 years as required for financial records.

4. Who sees what

Public: your profile, services, ratings, reviews, and (if verified) the badge. Non-public: your phone number is never displayed publicly; it is used for OTP verification and money-critical alerts. Dispute evidence (including message transcripts) is visible to the parties and to Skilvi support staff handling the case.

5. Your rights (NDPR)

You may request access to your data, correct it, export it, or ask for deletion — from Settings or by writing to privacy@skilvi.ng. Deletion is subject to our legal retention obligations (e.g., settled transaction records). You may withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time; transactional messages continue as required to operate your account.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies for sessions and a small amount of anonymous analytics to improve the platform. No advertising trackers, no third-party data sharing.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest for sensitive data, role-based access controls, two-factor authentication for staff, and an immutable audit log of staff actions on user data.

8. Contact

Data Protection Officer: privacy@skilvi.ng · Skilvi Technologies Ltd, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.
