Why we built this

Global platforms work against locals

USD payouts, delayed transfers, arbitrary account freezes — and competing against the whole world on price. A great PH developer or a great Lagos electrician shouldn't have to fight that fight.

Local work runs on WhatsApp

No profiles, no reviews, no payment protection. If the tiler disappears after your deposit, there is no one to call. Informal trust is real — but it doesn't scale.

Clients can't tell who's real

Whether it's a logo or a bathroom, the risk feels the same: you've never met this person. So deals stall, or money moves off-platform, unprotected.

How we keep both sides safe

Escrow on every order

Clients pay Skilvi, not the worker. Money is released only when the work is done and approved.

Verified identities

A one-time check of government ID + photo. The badge says who they are — never what we certified.

Real dispute mediation

Frozen funds, evidence, message history, and a 5-business-day resolution target.

Audited, accountable staff

Every admin action on money or accounts is written to an immutable audit log.

Simple money rules

Workers never pay to exist on Skilvi. We make money when you both succeed.
